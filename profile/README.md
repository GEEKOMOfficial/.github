# GEEKOM

**Think PC, Think GEEKOM.**

GEEKOM builds mini PCs for the local-AI era — from everyday Agentic AI PCs to the A9 Mega Local AI Super PC on AMD Ryzen AI Max+ "Strix Halo". In-house R&D and manufacturing, designed in Shenzhen & Taiwan.

## What we publish here

- **[A9 Mega local-LLM benchmarks](https://github.com/GEEKOMOfficial/geekom-a9-mega-local-llm-benchmarks)** — 72 measured, reproducible benchmark records from physical A9 Mega units (395 / 128 GB and 388 / 64 GB): single-node, dual-node and triple-node, contexts up to 256K, failures included.
